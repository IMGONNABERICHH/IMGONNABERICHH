7 L'ANGES is a creative media studio based in Los Angeles, built to document, amplify, and archive the artists and communities shaping culture from the ground up.

We specialize in film, music video, and cultural media — not as a service, but as a practice. Our work is rooted in Leimert Park, the LA underground, and the Black creative ecosystem that the rest of the industry follows years too late.

We are not for everyone. We work with a select number of artists, labels, and brands per year — the ones with something real to say and the commitment to say it correctly. If that's you, let's talk.
