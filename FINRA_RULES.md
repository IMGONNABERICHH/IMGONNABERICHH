Last updated: July 1, 2026

## Series 7 — General Securities Representative Exam

**Administered by:** FINRA  
**Format:** 125 scored questions + 5 unscored pretest questions (reduced from 10, effective Oct 27, 2025)  
**Passing score:** 72%  
**Time:** 3 hours 45 minutes  
**Prerequisite:** Must pass the SIE (Securities Industry Essentials) first

### Key Content Areas
- Equity securities
- Debt securities
- Options (puts, calls, spreads, hedges)
- Packaged products (mutual funds, ETFs, UITs, variable products)
- Retirement accounts and tax treatment
- Customer accounts and suitability
- Supervision and regulatory requirements

### 2025-2026 Updates
- Pretest questions reduced from 10 → 5 (Oct 27, 2025) — same scored content, same pass score
- CE (Continuing Education) 2026 focus areas: Reg BI enforcement, digital assets, cybersecurity, GenAI supervision

## Series 65 — Uniform Investment Adviser Law Exam (NASAA)

**Administered by:** NASAA (testing via FINRA Prometric centers)  
**Format:** 130 scored questions + 10 pretest questions  
**Passing score:** 92/130 correct (revised June 12, 2023, lowered from 94/130)  
**Time:** 3 hours  
**License:** Qualifies as Investment Adviser Representative (IAR)

### Content Sections (by weight)

| Section | Topic | Weight |
|---------|-------|--------|
| I | Economic Factors & Business Information | ~15% |
| II | Investment Vehicle Characteristics | ~25% |
| III | Client Investment Recommendations & Strategies | ~30% |
| IV | Laws, Regulations & Guidelines (incl. Fiduciary) | ~30% |

### Key Topics
- **Fiduciary duty** — IARs are fiduciaries; must act in client's best interest at all times
- **Suitability** — know your client, risk tolerance, time horizon, liquidity needs
- **Economics** — GDP, inflation, interest rate cycles, monetary/fiscal policy
- **Portfolio theory** — MPT, efficient frontier, alpha, beta, Sharpe ratio, standard deviation
- **Retirement planning** — IRAs, 401(k), RMDs, Roth conversions
- **Regulation** — Investment Advisers Act of 1940, state registration thresholds ($100M AUM)
- **Performance reporting** — GIPS standards, fee disclosure
- **Ethics** — fraud, misrepresentation, conflicts of interest

### 2023-2026 Updates
- Passing score lowered to 92/130 (June 2023)
- New testable topics added (June 2023): blockchain, crypto/digital tokens, SPACs, elderly
  client suitability protections
- SEC Investment Adviser Marketing Rule changes reflected in exam (effective Apr 2022)
- **IAR Continuing Education (2026):** 24 states now require 12 CE credits/year for IARs —
  6 credits Products & Practice + 6 credits Ethics & Professional Responsibility

## Major FINRA Rule Changes — 2026

### Rule 4210 — PDT Rule Eliminated (Effective June 4, 2026)
The $25,000 pattern day trader (PDT) minimum equity requirement is **gone**.

**What changed:**
- No more PDT designation — broker-dealers no longer track day trade counts
- No more $25,000 minimum equity requirement to day trade
- Minimum equity requirement is now just **$2,000** (existing margin rule baseline)
- Unlimited day trades allowed regardless of account size
- Intraday buying power is now based on **real-time intraday margin excess**
  (you must maintain 25% maintenance margin throughout the trading day)
- Phase-in period: firms have until October 20, 2027 to fully implement

**What this means for traders:**
- Small accounts can now day trade freely
- No more broker restrictions at 3 round-trips per week
- Leverage is still governed by maintenance margin (25% of current market value)
- You can trade with less than $2,000 but only with available cash, no leverage

### Rule 3290 — Outside Activities (Proposed, Q3 2026 Decision Expected)
- Would replace Rule 3270 (Outside Business Activities) and Rule 3280
  (Private Securities Transactions)
- Broadens disclosure requirements for registered reps doing outside work

## FINRA 2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report — Priority Areas

1. **Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI)** — ongoing enforcement focus; firms must
   document best-interest rationale for every recommendation; rollover
   recommendations heavily scrutinized
2. **Digital Assets** — FINRA continues to flag crypto custody, disclosure, and
   trading platform compliance; firms must monitor legislative/policy changes
3. **AI / GenAI Supervision** — firms must establish governance frameworks before
   deploying AI tools; top use case is document summarization/extraction;
   supervisory obligations apply to AI-generated communications
4. **Cybersecurity** — tied to Reg S-P (privacy), Reg S-ID (identity theft), Rule
   3110 (supervision), Rule 4370 (BCP); incident response plans required
5. **Small-Cap Fraud** — targeted examination program ongoing for exchange-listed
   small-cap equities
6. **Best Execution** — Rule 5310 compliance; firms must regularly review order
   routing and execution quality
7. **CAT (Consolidated Audit Trail)** — ongoing compliance with customer and order
   reporting obligations

## How This Applies to Our Trading Chain

| Rule/Concept | Relevance |
|---|---|
| PDT eliminated | Day trading RKLB, ASTS, etc. with small account is now legal without $25k min |
| Reg BI | If ever managing others' money, every trade recommendation must be in client's best interest |
| Fiduciary (Series 65) | IARs must put client interest first — same philosophy as Option B (no chasing, disciplined entry) |
| Suitability | Position sizing ($20 per trade) matches risk tolerance of a small test account |
| Digital assets on exam | Relevant to watchlist: NVDA (AI/crypto infrastructure), ASTS, AUR all adjacent |
